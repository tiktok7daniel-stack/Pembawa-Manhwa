      FAQ

      Which app opens CBZ files?

      Any comic reader: Tachiyomi/Mihon, Perfect Viewer, CDisplayEx, YACReader, Panels, Apple Books and more.

      Why did a site fail?

      Some sites hide chapters behind JavaScript, logins or protection against bots. Try the MangaDex link of the same series.

      Are my files kept on the server?

      Files are temporary and deleted automatically about 30 minutes after they are created.
